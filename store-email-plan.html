Subject lineThe first burn matters mostPreview textOne habit that keeps a candle burning evenly to the end.
A candle remembers its first burn. If you blow it out before the wax has melted all the way to the edge of the tin, it will tunnel down the middle every time after that, and a good part of the candle goes to waste.

So the first time you light one of ours, give it time. Let the melted wax reach the rim before you put it out. In an 8 oz tin that takes a while, so light it on an evening when you are staying in.

A couple of other habits help. Trim the cotton wick a little before each burn, and keep the candle out of a draft so the flame stays steady.

Each of our tins burns for about 40 hours when it is looked after this way. They come in lavender, sea salt and cedar, and we pour every one by hand in small batches here in Wilmington.
