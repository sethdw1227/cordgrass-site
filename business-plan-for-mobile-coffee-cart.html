A coffee cart’s sales depend on where it stands and for how many hours. The plan builds sales from cups per day at your spots and events, and shows what a slow season costs.

What you tell us

Where the sales come from. For example:

  • Daily service at a regular spot
  • Markets and events
  • Private and corporate bookings

For each one: what you expect in month one, by month twelve, and how fast it grows after that. Best estimates are fine.

What it costs to run. For example:

  • Coffee, milk and cups as a share of each sale
  • Event and pitch fees
  • Fuel or towing
  • Permits and insurance

 

What you need to buy to open. For example:

  • The cart or trailer and espresso machine
  • A generator or power set-up
  • Permits and opening stock

Who you will pay. A second barista, and the month they start.

The money you are starting with, and the plain facts: what you sell, where, to whom, who else does it, and what sets you apart.

What the plan works out

  • Sales, costs and profit for each of five years, and month by month
  • The month the business first shows a profit
  • The sales it takes to cover fixed costs
  • Cash at the end of every month, and its lowest point
  • What happens if sales come in 20% lower, 10% lower or 10% higher
  • If your numbers run out of cash, you are told before anything is written

What you get

  • A Word document of about 25 pages: fifteen chapters, financial statements, four charts and appendices
  • An industry and local market chapter built from government figures for your trade and your town, each with its source
  • An Excel workbook holding every figure
  • For $395: summary slides and one full redo if your numbers change

We are not accountants. The projections are estimates from your assumptions. They are not audited, reviewed or compiled, and no result is promised. This is a planning document; we do not prepare applications for financing or advise on borrowing, investing, tax or law. Software tools, including AI, draft and check the work, and a person reads it before it is sent.
