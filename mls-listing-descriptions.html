Questions

What do I need to send?

The property type, bedrooms, bathrooms, approximate size, and the features you want mentioned. Add anything about the setting or view. If your MLS has a character limit, include it.

Is it written by AI or by a person?

Both. Software tools, including AI, draft and check each description, and a person reads every one before it is sent to you.

Will it fit my MLS rules?

Each description is kept to the length you order and checked for wording that describes the property rather than the buyer. MLS rules differ by region, so give it a read against your own board's rules before you post.

Do you look at my photos or visit the property?

No. The description is written only from the facts you type, and those facts are not verified. If something matters, put it in your notes.

How fast is it?

Standard descriptions arrive by the end of the next day after your facts arrive. Long descriptions arrive within 2 days.

What if I want changes, or it is not usable?

Reply once with your changes and they are included. If the description still is not usable, you get your money back.

Plain promises

  • Only your facts are used. If something is missing, you are asked. Nothing is guessed.
  • A person reads every description before it is sent.
