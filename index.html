How it works

1. Send the facts

Pick what you need on the order page and type what you know. Rough notes are fine. If something is missing you get a question, never a guess.

2. It is written and checked

Software tools, including AI, draft the work and check every claim against what you sent. A person reads it before it goes out.

3. It arrives by email

Plain text or files, ready to use. Ask for changes by replying. If it is not usable, you get your money back.

Plain promises

  • Every claim about your business comes from the facts you send. General background is written by us. Outside figures in a business plan come from named government sources, and projections are calculated from your own numbers. No invented statistics, awards, prices or claims.
  • No results are promised: not rankings, not sales, not outcomes.
  • No contract on monthly work. Cancel by replying to any email.
  • If the work is not usable, you get your money back.
